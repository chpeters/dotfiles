# Readback

Restate the user's intended outcome, authorized scope, constraints, settled decisions and what would establish completion. Separate explicit instructions from your assumptions. Identify only ambiguities that could materially change the result, with a recommended interpretation where justified.

Use the user's vocabulary and concrete examples to expose misunderstandings. Do not turn the restatement into a new design, expand scope, or begin implementation. Keep straightforward requests brief. If asked to confirm alignment before action, stop at the readback and leave the decision to the user.
